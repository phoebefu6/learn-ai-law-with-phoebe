# Official course map - learn-ai-law-with-phoebe

**Course:** AI + Law - how lawyers embrace AI in practice
**Scope:** Broad legal practice (in-house + law firm): legal research, contract drafting, contract review/redlining, due diligence + document review, memos + briefs, client communication, litigation support, compliance/policy, and safe practice. Distinct from the gov bucket (PDPA/GDPR/AI-governance regulation) - this is how LAWYERS use AI to do legal work.
**Arc:** Topic-session, two-track: leader track (a1-a6, GC / partners / legal leaders: strategy, ethics, ops, risk) + practitioner track (b1-b10, hands-on lawyer: research, contracts, review, memos, litigation, compliance).
**Coverage bar:** ~80% of mapped sources' working content per session; certificates/CLE credit stay with the official programs.
**Build mode:** course-taking loop PAUSED (built direct from verified sources, no learner-notes step).
**IMPORTANT:** This course teaches AI skills for legal professionals. It is NOT legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Jurisdiction-specific rules of professional conduct vary - verify against your bar's rules.

## Market context (verify before delivery - moves fast)

- The skills gap is the story: ~80% of firms are investing in AI, but only ~35% feel their lawyers have adequate AI skills.
- Legal research was AI's first high-profile use AND its first public failure (2023 fabricated citations). Legal-specific tools reduce but do NOT eliminate hallucination - verification is a professional duty, not an option.
- The competence duty now includes competence with relevant technology, including AI.

## Hard rails (heaviest of the series - bake into every relevant page)

- **"AI assists, the licensed lawyer decides and owns it - and you verify every citation and fact against primary sources."** The 2023 fabricated-citations sanctions are the defining lesson: never file or rely on an AI output you have not independently verified. Competence + candor duties apply.
- **Confidentiality + privilege rail:** never put privileged or client-confidential information into a consumer AI tool; use vetted, confidential legal-AI tools with appropriate terms. Confidentiality duty (Model Rule 1.6) applies.
- **UPL rail:** AI is not a lawyer and does not give legal advice; do not let AI output substitute for a lawyer's judgment, and mind unauthorized-practice-of-law limits.
- **Not legal advice:** this course is education for legal professionals, not legal advice; rules of professional conduct are jurisdiction-specific - verify against your bar.

## Not covered by design (say so honestly)

- Data-protection / AI regulation (PDPA, GDPR, EU AI Act) -> the gov bucket courses.
- Jurisdiction-specific bar rules + CLE credit -> your bar + accredited providers.
- Building/fine-tuning legal ML models -> technical ML courses.
- Actual legal advice on any matter -> a licensed attorney.
