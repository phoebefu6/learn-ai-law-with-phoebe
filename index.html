Two tracks for lawyers who embrace AI

A leader track that teaches GCs and partners to steer AI in legal practice - ethics, risk, and legal ops - without a line of code, and a practitioner track that puts AI to work in the real craft of lawyering: research, contracts, review, memos, and litigation support. Around 80% of firms are investing in AI, but only about 35% of lawyers feel AI-ready. This closes that gap - carefully, with verification as a duty.

The one rule this whole course runs on: AI assists, the licensed lawyer decides and owns it - and you verify every citation and fact against primary sources. Never put privileged or client-confidential information into a consumer tool. This course is education for legal professionals, not legal advice, and rules of professional conduct vary by jurisdiction.
